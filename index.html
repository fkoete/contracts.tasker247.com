Master Services Agreement

  1. 1. Scope of Services

    The Service Provider agrees to render services as requested by the Client, including but not limited to administrative, compliance, business support, process improvement, data visualization, and related consulting services as outlined in written communication, quotation, proposal, or the applicable Service Annexure attached hereto.

  2. 2. Client Information Disclosure & Accuracy

    The Client warrants that all information, records, instructions, and documentation supplied to the Service Provider are accurate, complete, lawful, and truthful.

    The Service Provider shall not be held liable for any errors, statutory penalties, delays, or unsuccessful outcomes arising directly or indirectly from incorrect, incomplete, forged, or misleading information provided by the Client.

  3. 3. Verification of Information & Statutory Due Diligence

    All compliance-related, statutory, and submitted documentation is subject to rigorous verification prior to the commencement or lodgement of services.

    The Service Provider reserves the right to:

    • Delay or suspend service execution pending third-party or statutory verification; and
    • Reject or terminate the engagement where documentation is deemed insufficient, unverifiable, or non-compliant with applicable legislation.
  4. 4. Fees, Invoicing & Payment Terms

    A non-refundable deposit or agreed upfront payment is required prior to the commencement of any work. The remaining balance shall be payable strictly in accordance with the agreed quotation, milestone schedule, or upon presentation of invoice.

    Failure to make timely payment may result in immediate suspension or termination of services, and the withholding of deliverables or statutory submissions.

  5. 5. Refund & Work Allocation Policy

    In the event that services cannot be successfully completed due to the Client's failure to provide required information, unresponsiveness, or statutory non-compliance, the Service Provider shall:

    • Retain a reasonable portion of fees proportional to the professional hours, disbursements, and work already performed; and
    • Refund any unearned remaining balance at its reasonable discretion, less administrative, operational, and bank processing costs.
  6. 6. Confidentiality

    Both parties agree to treat all commercial, financial, technical, operational, and proprietary information disclosed during the course of this Agreement as strictly confidential.

    Neither party shall disclose confidential information to any third party without the prior written consent of the other, except where disclosure is required by law, court order, or strictly necessary for regulatory filings made on the Client's explicit instruction.

  7. 7. Protection of Personal Information (POPIA)

    Both parties acknowledge and agree to comply with all applicable provisions of the Protection of Personal Information Act No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA):

    • Roles: The Client is the Responsible Party and the Service Provider acts as an Operator processing personal information on the Client's written mandate.
    • Purpose Specification: The Service Provider shall process personal data solely for the explicit purpose of fulfilling the contracted services and statutory obligations.
    • Security Safeguards: The Service Provider shall maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure data integrity and safeguard against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction.
    • Retention & Deletion: Personal data shall not be retained longer than necessary to achieve the contracted purpose or meet statutory record-keeping mandates.
  8. 8. Intellectual Property

    All pre-existing Intellectual Property, proprietary methodologies, frameworks, and tools belonging to either party prior to this Agreement remain the exclusive property of that party.

    All customized deliverables, process maps, reports, and documentation developed specifically for the Client pursuant to this Agreement shall vest in the Client upon receipt of full payment.

    The Service Provider retains ownership of its underlying templates, standard operating frameworks, and proprietary business workflows.

  9. 9. Limitation of Liability

    To the fullest extent permitted by South African law, the Service Provider, its directors, employees, and agents shall not be liable for any indirect, special, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages, loss of profits, loss of data, or business interruption.

    The Service Provider's total aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, delict, or otherwise, shall be strictly limited to the total fees actually paid by the Client for the specific service giving rise to the claim.

  10. 10. Electronic Signatures & Digital Execution

    This Agreement may be executed through electronic signature in accordance with the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act No. 25 of 2002 (ECTA):

    • The parties consent to the use of electronic signatures, typed names, digital checkboxes, and online verification tools to signify mutual consent and execution.
    • The parties agree that electronic execution shall have the same legal force, validity, evidential weight, and enforceability as a physical handwritten signature.
    • Automated audit trail records (including IP addresses, timestamps, and browser verification) recorded upon submission constitute prima facie proof of execution.
  11. 11. Force Majeure

    Neither party shall be in breach of this Agreement or liable for delay or failure to perform obligations if caused by an event beyond its reasonable control (Force Majeure Event), including acts of God, civil commotion, strikes, power grid failures, telecommunication interruptions, pandemics, or statutory body shutdowns.

    Should a Force Majeure Event prevent performance continuously for more than 21 (twenty-one) business days, either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice.

  12. 12. Termination

    Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy such breach within 7 (seven) business days of written demand.

    Upon termination, the Service Provider shall be entitled to payment for all work performed up to the date of termination, and any applicable refunds will be processed pursuant to Clause 5.

  13. 13. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

    This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of South Africa.

    • Negotiation: Any dispute shall first be referred to good-faith negotiation between designated representatives within 7 (seven) business days of written notice.
    • Mediation & Arbitration: If unresolved within 14 (fourteen) business days, the dispute shall be submitted to administered mediation and/or binding arbitration under the rules of the Arbitration Foundation of Southern Africa (AFSA).
    • Urgent Relief: Nothing herein precludes either party from seeking urgent interim relief or an interdict from the High Court of South Africa.

Annexure A – Compliance Services

1. Scope of Work & Deliverables

  • CIPC & Statutory Filings: Facilitation and lodgement of annual returns, Director amendments (COR39), Company name reservations/changes, Incorporation of External Companies and MOI updates.
  • Tax & Statutory Status: Preparation and liaison for SARS Tax Compliance Status (TCS) PINs, VAT/PAYE registrations, and statutory clearance monitoring.
  • Labour & Compensation: Facilitation of Department of Employment and Labour submissions, COIDA registration, and Return of Earnings (ROE) lodgements.
  • B-BBEE Facilitation: Gathering verified ownership affidavits, supporting documentation, and EME/QSE certificate coordination.
  • Trust Registration and Secretarial Services: Facilitation of Trust registration with the Master of the High Court, including the preparation and submission of all required documentation, such as the Trust Deed and appointment of trustees. This service also includes ongoing statutory secretarial support for Trusts, ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and the maintenance of statutory records.
  • Trademark Registration and Secretarial Services: Facilitation of trademark registration with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC), including the preparation and submission of all required documentation, such as the trademark application form and supporting documentation. This service also includes ongoing statutory secretarial support for trademarks, ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and the maintenance of statutory records.
  • Strategic Compliance Guidance: Consultation services on industry-specific regulatory requirements, compliance risk assessments, and the development of internal control frameworks. This includes ad-hoc advisory support on compliance obligations, policy development, and the implementation of best practices to mitigate legal and regulatory risks.

2. Client Responsibilities & Dependencies

  • Provision of valid certified director/shareholder IDs, proof of residential/business address, and company resolutions within 5 business days.
  • Timely provision of verification OTPs and signing of statutory submission forms.

3. Disclaimers & Statutory Boundaries

Tasker247 acts solely as an administrative intermediary facilitating submissions based on Client-supplied records. Approval timelines, audits, and final statutory issuances remain strictly subject to the independent authority and backlogs of relevant regulatory bodies (CIPC, SARS, DoL).

Annexure F – Document Drafting, Development & Production

1. Scope of Work & Undertaking &Deliverables

  • A1. Definitions:
  • "Client Material" means all audio recordings, video recordings, handwritten documents, scanned images, and any other source material supplied by the Client for the purposes of the Services.
  • "Work Product" means all transcripts, digitized text, minutes, certificates, and other deliverables produced by the Service Provider.
  • "Back File" means the single retention copy of Client Material and Work Product held by the Service Provider for archival and retrieval purposes.

A2. Confidentiality of Client Material:

  • A2.1 In addition to Clause 6 of the Master Services Agreement, the Service Provider shall treat all Client Material as strictly confidential, shall use it solely to perform the Services, and shall not disclose it to any third party except where required by law.
  • A2.2 The Service Provider shall ensure that any employee, contractor, or sub-processor with access to Client Material is bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Annexure.

A3. Prohibition on AI Training:

  • A3.1 The Service Provider shall not use any Client Material or Work Product to train, fine-tune, refine, or otherwise improve any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, whether commercial or proprietary.
  • A3.2 Where AI-assisted tools (including OCR software and transcription assistance tools) are used, they shall be configured so that Client data is not retained by the tool provider for training or product development.
  • A3.3 On written request, the Service Provider shall confirm which AI-assisted tools were used and the data retention policies of those tools.

A4. Retention and Back File:

  • A4.1 The Service Provider may retain the Back File for 30 days after delivery of the final Work Product, solely to address errors, omissions, or queries.
  • A4.2 On expiry of the 30-day period, or on the Client's earlier written request, the Service Provider shall permanently delete Client Material and Work Product from live systems.
  • A4.3 Residual copies may persist in automated backups for a limited period and shall be aged out in accordance with the Service Provider's standard retention cycle.
  • A4.4 On written request, the Service Provider shall provide written certification of deletion from live systems.
  • A4.5 The Back File is not a permanent archive.

A5. Handwriting Digitization — Verification Standard:

  • A5.1 Where the Services include digitization of handwritten documents, the Service Provider shall process the source using OCR technology and shall then perform line-by-line human verification of all OCR output against the original image before delivery.
  • A5.2 No OCR output shall be delivered without such verification. Illegible or ambiguous words shall be marked [illegible] or with the Service Provider's best reading in brackets.

A6. Certificate of Compliance and Accuracy

  • A6.1 Every set of completed Work Product shall be accompanied by a signed Certificate of Compliance and Accuracy, confirming the scope of work performed, the quality checks run, the accuracy statement, the 7-day correction window, and the data-handling and retention terms in this Annexure.

A7. Conflict

  • In the event of conflict between this Annexure and the main body of the Agreement, this Annexure prevails in respect of data handling, confidentiality, retention, and AI training.
