Annexure A – Compliance Services

1. Scope of Work & Deliverables

  • CIPC & Statutory Filings: Facilitation and lodgement of annual returns, Director amendments (COR39), Company name reservations/changes, and MOI updates.
  • Tax & Statutory Status: Preparation and liaison for SARS Tax Compliance Status (TCS) PINs, VAT/PAYE registrations, and statutory clearance monitoring.
  • Labour & Compensation: Facilitation of Department of Employment and Labour submissions, COIDA registration, and Return of Earnings (ROE) lodgements.
  • B-BBEE Facilitation: Gathering verified ownership affidavits, supporting documentation, and EME/QSE certificate coordination.

2. Client Responsibilities & Dependencies

  • Provision of valid certified director/shareholder IDs, proof of residential/business address, and company resolutions within 5 business days.
  • Timely provision of verification OTPs and signing of statutory submission forms.

3. Disclaimers & Statutory Boundaries

Tasker247 acts solely as an administrative intermediary facilitating submissions based on Client-supplied records. Approval timelines, audits, and final statutory issuances remain strictly subject to the independent authority and backlogs of relevant regulatory bodies (CIPC, SARS, DoL).

Annexure B – Administrative Services

1. Scope of Work & Deliverables

  • Document Drafting & Filing: Preparation, standardisation, and secure cloud filing of administrative records, business letters, and client registers.
  • Secretarial & Operations Support: Diary coordination, dispatch of scheduled client/vendor communications, and meeting minutes documentation.
  • Billing & Record Reconciliation: Generating standard invoices, matching receipts to ledgers, and preparing transactional audit trails.

2. Client Responsibilities & Dependencies

  • Timely supply of clear source documents, transaction receipts, bank statements, and approval instructions.
  • Designation of an authorised representative for operational queries and approvals.

3. Disclaimers & Limitations

Administrative actions are performed strictly on client instructions. Tasker247 is not an auditing firm and does not provide formal statutory audits or fiduciary oversight beyond agreed administrative assistance.

Annexure C – Process Mapping & Business Improvement

1. Scope of Work & Deliverables

  • Operational Workflow Audit: Detailed discovery sessions to map current-state (As-Is) operational and transactional workflows.
  • Bottleneck & Efficiency Analysis: Identification of process waste, compliance risks, handoff delays, and automation opportunities.
  • SOP Formulation & To-Be Design: Drafting Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), process swimlane flowcharts, and future-state operational models.
  • Handover & Staff Enablement: Delivery of documented workflow manuals and walkthrough presentations to key stakeholders.

2. Client Responsibilities & Dependencies

  • Providing Tasker247 consultants access to operational team leads, workflow observations, and existing internal documentation.
  • Prompt feedback and review on draft process maps and SOP documentation.

3. Disclaimers

Recommendations reflect operational observations captured during the audit engagement. Tasker247 does not guarantee specific commercial or financial outcomes resulting from internal change management execution.

Annexure D – Data Visualisation & Reporting

1. Scope of Work & Deliverables

  • Data Ingestion & Cleaning: Extraction, cleansing, validation, and structuring of raw tabular and relational data sources.
  • Interactive Dashboard Design: Development of tailored business intelligence dashboards (KPI scorecards, operational trackers, executive summaries).
  • Automated Reporting Pipelines: Establishing refresh schedules, automated summary views, and export-ready presentation templates.

2. Client Responsibilities & Dependencies

  • Providing secure, timely access to verified source datasets, APIs, access tokens, and calculation logic definitions.
  • Ensuring all provided raw data complies with POPIA and applicable privacy regulations.

3. Disclaimers

Data visualization outputs and analytics rely entirely on the completeness, timeliness, and structural integrity of the source data provided by the Client.

Annexure E – Project & Consulting Services

1. Scope of Work & Deliverables

  • Project Structuring: Formulation of project charters, milestone schedules, work breakdown structures (WBS), and resource plans.
  • Execution & Monitoring: Ongoing tracking of project milestones, risk & issue register management, and stakeholder status reporting.
  • Consulting Advisory & Closeout: Strategic advisory sessions, deliverable quality sign-offs, and final project handover documentation.

2. Client Responsibilities & Dependencies

  • Timely stakeholder attendance at scheduled milestone checkpoints and steering committee meetings.
  • Formal sign-off on deliverables within 5 business days of submission.

3. Disclaimers

Project milestones are contingent upon agreed stakeholder review cycles. Material scope expansions require a mutually signed Change Order detailing fee and timeline adjustments.
